Applied Tariff:
This rate includes preferential tariff when it exists. This rate is normally lower than the MFN Tariff, except in few cases where some countries apply a Non-MFN tariff  which is higher than MFN Tariff.
A blank field of MFN Tariff/ Applied Tariff means that the rate was either not reported by the country or it is a specific tariff that could not be estimated using UNCTAD’s Advalorem Equivalent estimation method.
